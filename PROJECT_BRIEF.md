# Stride — project brief

A colourful, inviting personal study tracker, structured to support accounts later.

## Navigation

1. Dashboard: today's priorities, weekly progress, daily quest and XP.
2. Subjects: Criminology (red), Business (green), IT (blue), with individual subject detail pages.
3. Study planner: planned sessions in a weekly calendar.
4. Focus timer: timed sessions and breaks.
5. Assignments & exams: deadlines, priorities and completion.
6. Study history: sessions, notes and filters by subject, activity and topic.
7. Rewards: levels, achievements and personal milestone rewards.
8. Online Courses & MOOCs: course/provider links, modules, progress and certificates; separate totals from school study.

## Session logging

Record subject or online course, activity (Flashcards, Past papers, Note taking, Homework), duration, date, topic and optional notes. Allow manual entry and recording from the timer. Show activity breakdowns within subjects.

## Rewards

Award 1 XP per study minute when a session is saved. Support overall and subject progress, daily quests and weekly goals. Achievements: First Steps, Paper Trail (5 past-paper sessions), Total Recall (10 flashcard sessions), Balanced Week (all school subjects in a week), Topic Revisited (same topic on 3 distinct dates), Five Hours Strong (300 minutes). Course study contributes to overall XP. Missing a day never removes XP.

## Initial delivery assumptions

Static website suitable for GitHub hosting. Browser storage for personal data initially; no cross-device sync yet. Include export/import backup. No invented study history or completed achievements. Keep subject names alongside colours for clarity. Mobile-friendly navigation and forms.

## Collaboration

Provide concrete check-ins on navigation, visual design, logging and rewards. The user has requested regular opportunities to steer the website.
